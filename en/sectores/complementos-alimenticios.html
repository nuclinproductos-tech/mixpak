Process

How we work

From technical definition to finished supplement batch, with formula, dosage, format, line and traceability validation.

01

Technical briefing and NDA

We define the product, matrix, claims, channel goals, final format, expected volume and confidentiality requirements.

02

Development and validation

We adjust formula, prototype, stability, dosage and industrial feasibility before moving to line production.

03

Lote piloto

We check matrix behavior, dosage, filling, closure and presentation before scale-up.

04

Production and copacking

We manufacture, pack, label and condition the final batch with quality control and traceability.
